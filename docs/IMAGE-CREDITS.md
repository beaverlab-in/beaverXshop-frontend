# Placeholder image credits

These photographs are **temporary stand-ins**. They show generic, publicly licensed electronics hardware, **not the exact products BeaverLab sells**.

All images come from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/) (free-licence media). They were downscaled to at most 1200 px wide and re-encoded as JPEG; nothing else was changed.
Licences that require attribution (CC BY / CC BY-SA) are satisfied by this file; keep it with the images for as long as they are used, and delete the images (and rows) when they are replaced.
